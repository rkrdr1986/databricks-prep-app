Install on Android & iPhone

You can install this app directly on any Android phone or iPhone **without needing the Google Play Store or Apple App Store**!

Android Devices (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Open this website link in **Google Chrome**.
  2. Tap the **3 dots menu** () in the top right corner.
  3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install app"**.
  4. Confirm when prompted. An app icon will be added to your device apps list!
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this website link in **Safari**.
  2. Tap the **Share button** () at the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
  4. Tap **Add** in the top right.
